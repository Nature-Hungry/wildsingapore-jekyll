## History

In 1999, when the last remaining landfill on Singapore's mainland was exhausted, the Semakau Landfill was created by enclosing part of Pulau Semakau and all of a small adjacent island (Pulau Sakeng) with a rock bund. All of Singapore’s waste now ends up in this Landfill, i.e., ash from the incineration plants and all non-incinerable waste. The parts of Pulau Semakau that was not destroyed remains natural.

## Regions

### Western shore habitats (2.5km x 400m)

Sandy shores, seagrass meadows, rubble with fringe of sparse corals.

### Eastern shore habitats (1.5km x 100m)

Sandy shores, mudflats, rubble with fringe of sparse corals.

### In the middle (about 15ha)

Original mangroves with mudlobster mounds and [a possible hot spring](http://wildshores.blogspot.sg/2011/10/semakau-at-high-tide-with-hot-spring.html), coastal forest, secondary forest. 1° 12.432’N 103° 45.588’E 196ha (from [Google Earth](http://www.google.com/earth/index.html) and [Earth Point](http://www.earthpoint.us/Shapes.aspx))

## About the name

*Pulau*=Island; *Semakau*=unknown, but *semak*=scrub or undergrowth.

## Facilities

Facilities on the Semakau Landfill: Jetty with washing up facilities and public toilets. About 1.6km walk to entrance to the western shore. About 600m to the eastern shore.

## Conservation status

Listed for use as ‘Open Space’ in the [URA Master Plan 2008](https://www.google.com/search?q=http://www.ura.gov.sg/uramaps/%3Fconfig%3Dconfig_preopen.xml%26preopen%3DMaster%2520Plan), i.e., “Area to be used or intended to be used as open space”. Status not indicated in [Parks and Waterbodies Plan](http://www.ura.gov.sg/uramaps/?preopen=Special%20and%20Detailed%20Controls%20Plans%20%28SDCP%29). Access to Pulau Semakau is managed by the [National Environment Agency](http://app2.nea.gov.sg/index.aspx) that runs the [Semakau Landfill.](http://app2.nea.gov.sg/semakaulandfill.aspx)

## Current conservation activities

* On 16 Jul 05, Pulau Semakau was opened for nature-related recreational activities. Members of the public can now visit the island for birdwatching, sports fishing and intertidal walks.
* Since 1998, Pulau Semakau has been one of the survey sites of [underwater coral reef surveys](http://coralreef.nus.edu.sg/survey/data.htm) by [ReefFriends](http://www.bluewatervolunteers.org/reeffriends/) of [NParks](https://www.google.com/search?q=http://www.nparks.gov.sg/cms/index.php%3Foption%3Dcom_content%26view%3Darticle%26id%3D120%26Itemid%3D123) and the [Blue Water Volunteers](http://www.bluewatervolunteers.org/).
* Since 2006, [TeamSeagrass](http://teamseagrass.blogspot.sg/) has been monitoring [seagrasses at Pulau Semakau](http://www.seagrasswatch.org/Singapore.html).
* From 2009 to 2011 [Project Semakau](http://rmbr.nus.edu.sg/projectsemakau/) collected data on the biodiversity of Pulau Semakau.
* In 2011, the mudflats on Pulau Semakau was surveyed during the [Mega Marine Survey](http://megamarinesurvey.blogspot.sg/search/label/pulau%20semakau).
* [A book featuring the biodiversity of Pulau Semakau](http://wildshores.blogspot.sg/search/label/project-semakau-book) has been published by the National Environment Agency that manages the Landfill.
