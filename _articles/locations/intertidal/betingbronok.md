## About the name

*Beting* = Sandbar; *Bronok* = a specific sea cucumber species.

## Conservation status

Beting Bronok currently is not listed as anything in the [URA Master Plan 2025](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/maps/index.html), and the current Tekong land reclamation avoids Beting Bronok. It should be a long lasting reef.
