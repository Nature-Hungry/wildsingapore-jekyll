The Bukit Timah Nature Reserve is home to a primary forest, near the summit of the hill. If you visit Jungle Fall Trail, you will be able to see the primary forest.

## Habitat

Bukit Timah Nature Reserve is home to a Lowland Dipterocarp Forest, with the ridges of the hill being covered in _Shorea curtisii_

This place is great for experiencing Singapore's primeval and once most-abundant habitat.

Birds found in this place:
• Asian Fairy Bluebird
• Red-crowned Barbet
