Pulau Tekong was pubicly accessible up until 1987, after which it was restricted and the entire island would be fully utilised by the Singapore Armed Forces.

## Not Its Original Shape

Pulau Tekong has experienced incredibly extensive modification and its modern day form has lost plenty of its intertidal biodiversity.

## The Mangrove

Young National Servicemen of today can remember crossing the Sphinx Bridge, and especially the very foul smell that accompanied that bridge. The mangroves there are very extensive at more than 5 kilometres in length, it spans from Tekong's east to west coast.
