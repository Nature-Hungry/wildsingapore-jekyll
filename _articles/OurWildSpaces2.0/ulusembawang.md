## Ecological Significance

The southern edge of Ulu Sembawang is officially designated as part of the Central Catchment Nature Reserve (CCNR). Rainwater from this area forms a network of small streams that flow into the Sungei Mandai River, eventually leading into Mandai Mangroves. This transition of habitat from an inland forest to mudflat and mangroves is extremely rare in Singapore. Moreover, this land-to-sea connection also forms a corridor that facilitates movement of aquatic fauna.

Although the site is mostly exotic-dominated beyond the nature reserve area, its immense size provides an important **buffer** around the CCNR against **edge effects**. Additionally, the Ulu Sembawang forest may act as a biodiversity ‘sink’ that allows for wildlife movement northwards from the nearby CCNR, demonstrating high restoration potential. The value of this forest is also highlighted in the **Singapore Terrestrial Conservation Plan** (2024).

## Why is This Area Threatened?

The area west of Bukit Timah Expressway is zoned as a Reserve site while the area east of Bukit Timah Expressway has a segment zoned as Nature Reserve. Most of Ulu Sembawang is zoned as Open Space, which is more commonly known as ‘Lorong Asrama’ and is used for military training. While its status as military land currently affords the forest ‘protection’ from other land-uses, mounting pressures for more housing and industry, and ongoing consolidation of military training areas may mean the development of places like Ulu Sembawang.

![](https://images.naturehungry.sg/photos/OWS2/Ulu-Sembawang-7.jpg)

## Our Hopes for the Site

The conservation of the site’s southern border should be prioritised to **buffer** the nature reserve portion from development. Further, the **headwaters** of Sungei Mandai should be preserved and buffered. The area could possibly be designated as a new ‘Sungei Mandai Nature Park’ with the potential **naturalisation** of Sungei Mandai through Kranji Racecourse.

Together, these areas could form a continuous ecological corridor linking Mandai Mangroves and Kranji Racecourse to Mandai T15 and CCNR, extending eastwards towards Sembawang Air Base.
