## Nature "Versus" Conservation

The idea of conservation and nature having direct conflict is false. Especially in the Singaporean context. Development can always happen on a brownfield site, and development can enhance brownfield sites to turn them into young secondary forests. An example of the latter can be found in Admiralty Park.

## Reforestation

{% include img-wrap-group.html items=page.galleries.gallery1 %}

This place was once a sparse exotic-dominated secondary forest, dominated by the *Falcataria falcata*. After plenty of tree plantings and habitat enhancement, the new man-made secondary forest was native-dominated. But the process is very labour-intensive. Regular grass trimmings, regular root protection replacements, and lots of manpower.

Reforestation is no joke, and the amount of money spent is immense.
