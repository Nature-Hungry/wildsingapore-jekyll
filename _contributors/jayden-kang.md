Jayden has been doing most of the outreach and planning for this website.

Jayden started birdwatching in the Central Catchment Nature Reserve with Jun Ze in 2021. They visit the reserve almost weekly ever since. He is familiar with Malayan forests, from Lowland Dipterocarp Forests to Hill Dipterocarp Forests, Jayden has seen plenty of trees and birds.
