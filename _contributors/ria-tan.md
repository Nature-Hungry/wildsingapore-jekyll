Ria Tan started her conservation journey about 30 years ago. It all began with the website [Wild Singapore](wildsingapore.com). Starting small, she added bits of content over time. After three decades, the end result was a very large website acting as a massive repository of information regarding all aspects of our Singaporean shores.

Without Ria, the conception of this website would not be possible. She still guides the Nature Hungry team and steers this project.
