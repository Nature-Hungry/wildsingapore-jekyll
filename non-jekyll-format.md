Copy and paste the text below at the top of your document. The text is structured in `<key> : <value>` pair, only modify the `<value>` part of the pair.

```
---
title: <Common name>
description: <Summary of the entry. Can be identification features or a general description of the species/group>
zone: 
- marine
- intertidal

- coral reef

- sand

subfamily: ""

rdb3: <capitalized 2 letter code>

---
```
